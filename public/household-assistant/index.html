Household assistant

This is a private tool that one family uses to keep track of bills, deadlines and reminders. It runs on a computer in the family's home. It is not a public service, and nobody outside the household can sign up for it.

Each person who uses it chooses to connect their own Google account. The tool then reads that person's mail and calendar entries to find items that need attention. It cannot send mail or change anything in the account.
