Household assistant privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers the Household assistant, a private application used by the members of one household. Only people the household invites can use it.

What it accesses

With each member's permission, the application reads that member's Gmail messages and Google Calendar events. It asks for two read-only permissions. One lets it view email messages and settings. The other lets it view calendars. It cannot send mail, change mail, or change calendar entries.

How it uses that information

The application looks for bills, deadlines and tasks in mail and calendar entries. It shows what it finds to the member who owns the account. Next to each item it shows a short excerpt of the message, so the member can check the item against the original.

Where the information is stored

Everything runs on a computer in the household's home. The permissions that let the application read an account are stored in encrypted form on that computer. The text of each message is deleted after it has been processed. For each item the application keeps the message identifier, a fingerprint of the message and a short excerpt.

Sharing

The application does not sell Google user data. It does not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Mail and calendar content is processed by software models that run on the household's own computer. It is not sent to any outside service.

Who can see it

A member sees only their own mail-derived items, plus anything they choose to share with the household. The household administrator maintains the computer and can technically reach stored data. Members agree to this when they connect their account.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The application's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The application does not use this information to train models.

Your choices

A member can disconnect at any time from the permissions page of their Google account, at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting ends the application's access straight away. A member can also ask the household administrator to delete the data stored about them.

Changes to this policy

Any change will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Members can contact the household administrator who invited them.
